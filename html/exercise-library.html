Upper Body Exercises

Push-Up

Chest Shoulders Triceps

A bodyweight push that builds pressing strength.

Benefits:

  • Builds chest, shoulder, and arm strength
  • Improves core stability
  • Can be done anywhere
  • Multiple variations for all fitness levels

How to Perform:

  1. Start in a tall plank with hands stacked under shoulders and core braced.
  2. Lower your chest toward the floor, keeping elbows around a 45° angle.
  3. Press evenly through both palms to return to the starting position.

Pull-Up

Back Biceps Shoulders

Simple vertical pull that targets the back and arms.

Benefits:

  • Develops strong back muscles
  • Builds functional upper body strength
  • Improves grip strength
  • Great for posture improvement

How to Perform:

  1. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width with palms facing away.
  2. Drive elbows down and back while pulling your chest toward the bar.
  3. Lower slowly until arms are straight while keeping shoulders engaged.

Dips

Triceps Chest Shoulders

Bodyweight dip variation to target triceps and chest.

Benefits:

  • Targets triceps effectively
  • Builds pushing strength
  • Strengthens shoulders and chest
  • Improves lockout strength

How to Perform:

  1. Support yourself on parallel bars with elbows locked and chest tall.
  2. Lower until elbows reach roughly 90° while keeping shoulders down.
  3. Press back to the top without snapping the elbows.

Lower Body Exercises

Squat

Quads Glutes Core

Foundational squat pattern for strong legs and core.

Benefits:

  • Builds powerful legs and glutes
  • Improves mobility and flexibility
  • Strengthens core and back
  • Functional movement pattern

How to Perform:

  1. Stand tall with feet shoulder-width and toes slightly out.
  2. Push hips back, bend knees, and lower until thighs are parallel with the floor.
  3. Keep chest lifted and drive knees in line with toes.
  4. Press through the heels to stand, squeezing glutes at the top.

Lunges

Quads Glutes Balance

Walking lunge to train single-leg strength and balance.

Benefits:

  • Corrects muscle imbalances
  • Improves balance and coordination
  • Builds leg and glute strength
  • Functional for daily activities

How to Perform:

  1. Step forward and lower until both knees reach roughly 90°.
  2. Keep torso upright and front knee stacked over the ankle.
  3. Drive through the front heel to stand and alternate legs.

Deadlift

Hamstrings Glutes Back

Heavy hinge emphasising glutes, hamstrings, and back.

Benefits:

  • Builds total body strength
  • Strengthens posterior chain
  • Improves posture and core stability
  • Highly functional movement

How to Perform:

  1. Stand with feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot, and hinge to grip it.
  2. Brace, drive the floor away, and extend hips and knees together.
  3. Lower with control while keeping the bar close to the body.

Core Exercises

Plank

Core Shoulders Stability

Static plank hold to train trunk stability.

Benefits:

  • Builds core endurance
  • Improves posture
  • Strengthens entire trunk
  • Low impact on spine

How to Perform:

  1. Set forearms on the floor with elbows under shoulders and legs extended.
  2. Squeeze glutes and keep a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Hold while breathing steadily and avoid letting hips sag.

Russian Twists

Obliques Core Balance

Rotational core move to challenge obliques.

Benefits:

  • Targets obliques effectively
  • Improves rotational strength
  • Enhances core stability
  • Functional for sports

How to Perform:

  1. Sit tall with knees bent, lean back slightly, and lift feet if possible.
  2. Hold a light weight at chest level and brace the core.
  3. Rotate torso side to side, touching the weight toward each hip.

Mountain Climbers

Core Cardio Full Body

Dynamic plank variation for cardio and core.

Benefits:

  • Cardio and strength combined
  • Burns calories efficiently
  • Improves core stability
  • Increases heart rate

How to Perform:

  1. Start in a strong plank with hands under shoulders.
  2. Drive one knee toward the chest while keeping hips level.
  3. Switch legs swiftly in a running motion while maintaining control.

Cardio Exercises

Burpees

Full Body Cardio Explosive

Full-body conditioning move that blends strength and cardio.

Benefits:

  • Full-body workout
  • Burns maximum calories
  • Improves cardiovascular fitness
  • Builds explosive power

How to Perform:

  1. From standing, drop into a squat and plant your hands.
  2. Kick feet back to a plank, perform a push-up if desired.
  3. Jump feet back in and explode upward with a small jump.

Jumping Jacks

Cardio Full Body Coordination

Classic warm-up drill to raise heart rate quickly.

Benefits:

  • Increases heart rate quickly
  • Improves coordination
  • Warms up entire body
  • No equipment needed

How to Perform:

  1. Start with feet together and arms by your sides.
  2. Jump feet out while raising arms overhead.
  3. Return to the starting position and repeat rhythmically.

High Knees

Cardio Legs Core

Running-in-place drill to build leg turnover and endurance.

Benefits:

  • Boosts cardiovascular fitness
  • Strengthens hip flexors
  • Improves running form
  • Burns calories effectively

How to Perform:

  1. Stand tall with arms bent at 90°.
  2. Drive one knee toward the chest as you rise onto the opposite toe.
  3. Alternate legs quickly while pumping the arms.
