Weekly Workout Plan

Structure your fitness routine for optimal results

Sample Weekly Schedule - Intermediate Plan

Weekly training plan overview
Day Workouts & Notes
Monday Upper Body Push
Bench Press or Push-Ups

4 sets × 8-12 reps | Focus on controlled movement and full range of motion

Overhead Shoulder Press

3 sets × 10-12 reps | Keep core tight, press dumbbells or barbell overhead

Incline Dumbbell Press

3 sets × 10-12 reps | Target upper chest with controlled tempo

Tricep Dips or Cable Pushdowns

3 sets × 12-15 reps | Isolate triceps for complete upper body push work

Tuesday Lower Body
Barbell or Goblet Squats

4 sets × 10-12 reps | Keep chest up, knees tracking over toes, full depth

Romanian Deadlifts

4 sets × 10-12 reps | Hinge at hips, feel hamstring stretch, maintain neutral spine

Walking Lunges

3 sets × 10 reps each leg | Maintain upright posture, control the descent

Leg Press or Bulgarian Split Squats

3 sets × 12 reps | Drive through heels, controlled movement

Calf Raises

3 sets × 15-20 reps | Full range of motion, squeeze at the top

Wednesday Active Recovery
Light Cardio Activity

20-30 minutes of walking, swimming, or easy cycling to promote blood flow and recovery

Stretching & Mobility Work

15-20 minutes of full body stretching, yoga, or foam rolling to improve flexibility

Optional: Core Stability

Light core work: planks, dead bugs, bird dogs – 2-3 sets of 30-45 seconds

Thursday Upper Body Pull
Pull-Ups or Lat Pulldowns

4 sets × 8-10 reps | Build back width, control the negative

Bent-Over Barbell or Dumbbell Rows

4 sets × 10-12 reps | Pull to lower chest, squeeze shoulder blades together

Face Pulls

3 sets × 15 reps | Target rear delts and improve posture

Bicep Curls

3 sets × 12 reps | Control the weight, focus on the squeeze

Hammer Curls

3 sets × 12 reps | Target brachialis for complete arm development

Friday Lower Body & Core
Deadlifts (Conventional or Sumo)

4 sets × 6-8 reps | Focus on form, drive through heels, powerful hip extension

Front Squats or Leg Press

3 sets × 10-12 reps | Keep chest up, controlled descent

Leg Curls

3 sets × 12-15 reps | Isolate hamstrings, control the eccentric

Core Circuit

Plank 45-60s, Russian Twists 20 reps, Hanging Leg Raises 12 reps – 3 rounds

Saturday HIIT or Cardio
HIIT Circuit Option

40 seconds work, 20 seconds rest × 8 rounds: Burpees, Mountain Climbers, Jump Squats, High Knees

Alternative: Steady-State Cardio

30-45 minutes running, cycling, rowing, or swimming at moderate, sustainable pace

Cool Down

10 minutes easy pace followed by full body stretching routine

Sunday Rest Day
Complete Rest & Recovery

Focus on sleep, nutrition, hydration, and mental recovery. Prepare for the week ahead.

Optional Gentle Movement

Light yoga, easy walk, or gentle stretching if you feel energized – keep it very low intensity

Meal Prep & Planning

Prepare healthy meals for the upcoming week to stay on track with nutrition goals
